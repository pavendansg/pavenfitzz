Sign in with Google in one tap, or create a new account with your name, email and a password of your choice. No OTP, no verification delays — you're in immediately.

● Free · One time only
Set up your profile

Enter your age, height, weight, gender, and fitness goal (Bulk / Cut / Maintain / Athletic). This is a one-time setup — your details are saved permanently so you never have to re-enter them.

Age · Height · Weight Fitness goal Saved forever

● Free · Every login
Daily check-in — BMI

Every time you log in, enter your current weight. The app instantly calculates your BMI, shows your category (Underweight / Healthy / Overweight), ideal weight range, and gives you specific tips for your situation.

Live BMI calculation Category + tips Ideal weight range

Based on your weight, height, age and goal, the app calculates your exact daily calorie target using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula. Bulk, cut, or maintain — your number is always precise, never a rough estimate.

See your exact protein, carbs, and fat targets in grams — calculated for your body and goal. A sample diet plan is shown so you know exactly what to eat. This data is saved to Firestore so your progress chart updates live.

View your weight and BMI trends over time as live line charts. See your streak milestones, goal completion percentage, and motivational messages. The more you check in, the richer this gets.

Questions

Need to know
before you start?

Everything you'd want to ask, answered.

Is EvolveFit really free to start?

+

Yes. Sign up, complete setup, and use BMI tracking, calorie targets, macros, and your progress graph completely free. Premium (₹5, one-time) unlocks the AI Workout, Diet, and Meal tools plus the full premium dashboard.

What exactly does the ₹5 premium unlock?

+

Lifetime access to the AI Workout Planner, Body Type Diet Analysis, AI Meal Generator, and the full Premium Dashboard with streak rank tracking. It's a one-time payment — no subscription, no renewal.

How does the streak and rank system work?

+

Every consecutive daily check-in adds to your streak. Hit milestones — 7, 30, 90, 180 days — to rank up from Beginner to Elite. Miss a day and your streak resets, so consistency is everything.

Do I need to re-enter my details every time?

+

No. Setup only happens once. Your details are saved permanently — you only update your weight during daily check-ins, and that updates everywhere instantly.

Can I use EvolveFit on my phone?

+

Yes, EvolveFit is fully responsive and mobile-first. No app download needed — just open it in any browser.

I already paid — why is it asking me to pay again?

+

This can happen if you cleared your browser cache or switched devices. Simply log in again and your premium status is automatically restored from our database — no need to pay twice.
