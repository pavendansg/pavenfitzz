🍗 BMR — Calories your body burns just existing (breathing, organs working) if you stayed in bed all day.

🔥 TDEE — Your BMR plus calories burned from daily activity and exercise. This is what you actually burn in a normal day.

🎯 Your Target — TDEE adjusted for your goal: a surplus to bulk, a deficit to cut, or matched exactly to maintain.
